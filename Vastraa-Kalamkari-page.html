Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Our MOQ starts from 25 meters for silk and 50 meters for cotton, linen, rayon, and canvas fabrics. We accommodate smaller orders for new clients wanting to test quality.
Do you provide fabric samples?
Yes, we provide fabric samples before bulk orders. Sample charges may apply and are adjustable against bulk orders.
Can you make custom Kalamkari prints?
Absolutely! We offer full customization including exclusive design development, Pantone color matching, and private label branding.
What is the production lead time?
Standard lead time is 7–15 days depending on fabric type and order size. Rush orders can be accommodated with prior discussion.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we export to 25+ countries worldwide including the US, UK, Europe, Middle East, and Southeast Asia via trusted logistics partners.
