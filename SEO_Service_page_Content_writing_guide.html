The exact section order proven to rank and convert. Every section has a job — do not skip or swap them. Google ranks pages, not websites. Each service needs its own dedicated page.
01

Hero — Keyword H1 + Benefit Subheadline + CTA

Your H1 must include your primary keyword and location or niche. Instead of "What We Do" write "SEO Services for Real Estate Businesses in Chennai." Your subheadline states the outcome, not the feature. CTA must be visible above the fold — no scrolling required.

Primary keyword in H1 Outcome-led subhead CTA above the fold
02

3 Key Benefits — Outcomes, Not Features

Benefit-focused copy converts 20–40% better than feature-focused copy. Use a 3-column layout: icon + heading + one-line outcome. Answer "what do I get?" not "what do you do?"

03

Pain Point + Validation Block

Name the exact frustration your client has before finding you. Show you understand it. This is where most visitors decide to keep reading — or leave. Be specific, not generic.

04

Service Details + Process Breakdown

Explain the what and how. Deep enough to answer "what exactly happens when I hire you?" Use H2s for each step so Google can understand topical depth and index you for multiple related queries.

H2 per step LSI keywords in subheads 800–1800 words minimum
05

Social Proof — Testimonials + Case Studies

Pick testimonials that reference a specific problem solved. Case studies must show a measurable ROI. Place near your "Why Choose Us" section to support every claim you make.

Google / Clutch verified reviews Before/after result data
06

FAQ Section — Objection Busting

Address concerns about cost, results, timeline, and reliability directly. Every FAQ answer is a chance to rank for a long-tail query and extend dwell time significantly. Always add FAQ schema markup.

07

Final CTA + Contact Block

Recap the core benefit using your primary keyword again. Make the next step dead simple. Minimal form fields. Phone number visible. Repeat CTA after every major section — not just at the bottom.

3 form fields maximum Phone visible in header CTA repeated mid-page
Important: Google ranks individual pages, not websites. A generic single "Services" page will never rank well. Each service needs its own URL, its own keyword focus, and its own full content build.
Dwell time = the time between a SERP click and returning to Google. A 2024 Google API leak confirmed it tracks "long click" behaviour. Longer dwell signals content matched the searcher's intent — and that likely influences rankings.

Do These Things

  • Embed a short explainer or process video — adds 1+ minute per viewer on average
  • Add an interactive element: ROI calculator, quiz, or comparison tool
  • Use internal links to related blog posts or sub-service pages
  • Build a topic cluster: pillar page → spoke pages → related blogs
  • Break long content with custom visuals, infographics, and diagrams
  • Write a strong intro using the PPT formula (see Copy tab)
  • Add a sticky table of contents for long pages
  • Include a scannable TL;DR / key takeaways box at the top

Avoid These Mistakes

  • Thin content under 800 words on a competitive service page
  • Content that does not match the searcher's intent
  • Pop-ups or interstitials that fire immediately on page load
  • Page speed over 3 seconds — this is the biggest dwell time killer
  • Walls of unbroken text with no visual hierarchy or breathing room
  • Outdated stats, broken links, or stale case study data
Real result — LearnWell EdTech: Added interactive quizzes, polls, and embedded instructor videos to long-form guides. Dwell time jumped from 42 seconds → 2 minutes 15 seconds. Bounce rate dropped 38%. Five blog posts moved into Google's top 5 for their target keywords.

Proven writing frameworks used by top-ranking service pages. These are not theory — they are tested formulas that increase both dwell time and conversion. Use them section by section.

PPT Formula — For Your Intro

The first 3 lines of any page carry the most weight for dwell time. Brian Dean (Backlinko) calls this the highest-impact intro framework:

Preview — Line 1: tell them exactly what this page delivers
Proof — Lines 2–3: show credibility immediately
Transition — Walk them gently into the next section
"This page explains how we've helped 40+ Chennai businesses rank on page 1 within 90 days — and exactly how we would do it for yours."

PAS Formula — For Pain Point Sections

Use this in section 3 of your page to create emotional resonance before presenting your solution:

Problem — Name the exact frustration ("You're spending on ads but SEO brings nothing")
Agitate — Show the real cost of doing nothing about it
Solution — Position your service as the specific, logical answer

E-E-A-T Signals to Weave Throughout

Google's quality raters look for Experience, Expertise, Authority, and Trust. Your copy must demonstrate all four — not just claim them:

  • Mention specific client results with numbers — Experience
  • Reference your process, tools, and methodology — Expertise
  • Include certifications, awards, or media mentions — Authority
  • Show verified reviews, clear pricing, and team photos — Trust

Keyword Placement Rules — Natural, Not Stuffed

  • Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, and in the final CTA
  • 3–5 LSI and secondary keywords placed naturally in H2s and body copy
  • Location keyword in meta title, H1, and at least one H2
  • Long-tail keywords in FAQ section answers for rich results
  • Keyword-relevant alt text on every image on the page
  • URL slug: short, clean, keyword-rich, hyphen-separated, no stop words
SEO brings the traffic. CRO converts it. When both are aligned, they reduce cost-per-lead, improve lead quality, and increase ROI without increasing ad spend.

Trust Signals That Convert

  • Client logo strip with recognisable brand names
  • Exact review count and star rating from Google or Clutch
  • Named case study with a clear percentage improvement
  • Team photos with names and roles — not stock images
  • "Featured in" or certification badges displayed prominently
  • Free consultation offer or money-back guarantee

CTA Best Practices

  • CTA appears above fold, after benefits, and at the page end
  • Use specific action text: "Get free SEO audit" not "Submit"
  • Reduce form fields to 3 or fewer for maximum submissions
  • Add micro-copy below CTA to remove risk ("No commitment")
  • Sticky CTA button on all mobile screen sizes
  • A/B test your main headline — can yield +20–50% conversion lift

Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links keep users exploring and signal topical depth to Google. Build your links like a map — every page connects to something relevant:

Service pages → location pages Location pages → top services Blog posts → money pages FAQs → deep-dive articles
One agency saw session depth go from 1.6 → 3.4 pages per visit and bounce rate drop from 62% → 41% using a topic cluster and internal linking strategy alone.
Use this before publishing every service page. Every item has a direct impact on rankings, dwell time, or conversion. If you can tick all of these, the page is ready to go live.

Content

  • Dedicated individual page per service — not one combined "Services" page
  • Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, meta title, URL, and final CTA
  • 800+ words minimum — 1,200–1,800 words recommended for competitive terms
  • Benefit-focused headline above the fold — outcome, not feature
  • Pain point section with specific, relatable language
  • Clear process explanation: what happens after they hire you
  • FAQ section with 5+ questions using long-tail keyword variations
  • At least one case study or testimonial with a measurable result
  • Video embedded: explainer, testimonial, or process walkthrough

SEO Technical

  • Meta title: primary keyword + brand name, under 60 characters
  • Meta description: benefit-led sentence with a CTA, under 155 characters
  • URL slug: clean, keyword-rich, no dates or stop words
  • All images have descriptive alt text with a relevant keyword
  • FAQ schema and LocalBusiness schema markup implemented
  • Page loads under 3 seconds on a mobile connection
  • Internal links to 2+ related pages with descriptive anchor text
  • Canonical tag set correctly to avoid duplicate content issues

Conversion

  • CTA appears above fold, mid-page after benefits, and at the end
  • Contact form has 3 fields or fewer
  • Phone number is visible in the header and at least once in the page body
  • Trust signals present: reviews, client logos, certifications
  • Mobile layout tested — sticky CTA button is active and visible
  • Page is tracked in GA4 with scroll depth and form submission events
After publishing: Open GA4 → filter Organic traffic → find this page → monitor Engagement rate, Average engagement time, and Conversions weekly. A page with high impressions but low dwell time needs the content revised — not more backlinks.
