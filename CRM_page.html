What are the 4 types of CRM software?

+
The four main types are: Operational CRM (automates sales, marketing, and service workflows), Analytical CRM (turns customer data into actionable insights), Collaborative CRM (aligns multiple departments around shared customer data), and Strategic CRM (focuses on long-term customer lifetime value). Rankraze CRM combines operational and analytical capabilities in a single platform, making it practical for SMEs that don't want to manage multiple tools.

What is CRM software and what does it do?

+
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. CRM software is a platform that centralises everything you know about your customers and prospects — contact details, communication history, deals, support tickets, and more — in one accessible system. It helps sales teams follow up consistently, marketing teams run targeted campaigns, and service teams resolve issues faster. The net result is better customer experience and higher revenue.

What is the difference between ERP and CRM software?

+
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) manages internal business operations — finance, inventory, HR, and production. CRM focuses on customer-facing activities — lead management, sales pipelines, marketing, and customer service. Many growing businesses start with CRM (which has a faster ROI) and add ERP modules later. Rankraze CRM can integrate with popular ERP systems via API if you already have one in place.

Can CRM software be used for personal use or by very small teams?

+
Absolutely. Freelancers, independent consultants, and solo founders use CRM software to track client conversations, proposals, and renewals. Rankraze CRM's entry plan is designed to be lightweight for individuals while scaling to larger teams without requiring a platform switch.
